Fearless Wallet is open-sourced, non-custodial, and decentralized mobile wallet designed with security in mind.

No other application on your mobile device can access Fearless Wallet during account creation.

Your mnemonic phrase is protected by a PIN, ensuring its safety even if your device is compromised.

You can read more about security [here](https://fearlesswallet.medium.com/security-in-fearless-wallet-773151bf64cb).
