Fearless Wallet has integrated MoonPay and RAMP, allowing you to purchase DOT and KSM directly within the app.

1. Go to the "Wallet" tab.

2. Select the token and the network, then click "Buy."

3. Choose MoonPay or RAMP and follow the steps to complete your purchase.

That's it! You've successfully bought DOT or KSM using Fearless Wallet.
