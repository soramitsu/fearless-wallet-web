---
question: How do I import my Fearless Wallet?
answer: To import your wallet, open the Import Menu, choose the import method (Mnemonic Phrase, Raw Seed, or JSON File), and follow the prompts to enter your information.
---

1. Open the Import Menu:

   - If you already have an account, click on your current account name in the "Wallet" section and select "Add Wallet".
   - If you are a new user, click on "Import Wallet" on the welcome screen.

2. Choose the Import Method:

   - Select one of the three options: Mnemonic Phrase, Raw Seed, or JSON File.

**Import Options:**

Mnemonic Phrase:

- Enter each word of the phrase in the correct order. Click "Continue," give your account a name, and set a new password.

Raw Seed:

- Paste the raw seed and click "Continue." Optionally, add parachain accounts. Then, name your account and set a new password.

JSON File:

- Select "Restore JSON," upload the encrypted file, and enter the original password. If needed, add EVM accounts.

After completing these steps, your account will appear in the "Wallet" section and will be ready for use!

Read the article: [Exporting and Importing a Wallet Using a Passphrase](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/accounts/walkthrough/exporting-and-importing-a-wallet-using-a-passphrase)
